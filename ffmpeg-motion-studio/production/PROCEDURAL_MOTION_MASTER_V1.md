# Production Concept — Dari Kasir ke Dalam Pikiran Komputer
## Procedural Motion Master v1.0

### Creative decision
Move away from a passive slideshow and generic character acting. The film becomes one connected transformation: a receipt turns into character groups, lexical tokens, grammar rules, a parse tree, an AST, and a tested computation pipeline. Every visual transition must explain a concept rather than decorate the narration.

### Visual grammar
- Deep ink-blue space, fine grid, restrained cyan/violet/amber accents, high-contrast ivory typography, and deliberate negative space.
- All art is programmatically drawn geometry, token pills, syntax nodes, connecting lines, and typography.
- No source photographs, stock pictures, imported storyboard images, reference images, or image-to-video inputs.
- No pointing character or animated hands. The explanatory objects are the subject, avoiding anatomy distortion.
- Motion uses deterministic easing, brief transition dips, progressive diagram reveals, and a traveling parser marker.
- The only valid transaction value is Rp54.000 − Rp5.400 = Rp48.600. Never display interpolated intermediate amounts as if they were a real total.

### Edit and sound
- Runtime 06:30; 27 scenes; 24 fps. Twelve scenes last 15 seconds and fifteen last 14 seconds, for 390 seconds.
- Indonesian neural TTS is attempted first. Local TTS fallback is explicitly recorded per scene and does not equal approved professional narration.
- A procedurally synthesized harmonic ambient bed runs across the film, ducking under narration; audio is normalized, limited, and checked for stream duration and non-silence.
- Captions are burned into the designed layout; a separate Indonesian SRT and transcript are also delivered.
- Human listening QC must still assess naturalness, pronunciation, pacing, intelligibility, and music balance.

### Automated acceptance gates
1. 27 unique scenes; 390-second timeline; exactly 9,360 frames at 24 fps.
2. Canonical receipt arithmetic remains correct.
3. Every scene has narration and a valid visual motif.
4. Narration and ambient audio are prepared before the video render.
5. Final MP4 contains H.264 1920×1080 at 24 fps and AAC stereo at 48 kHz; both streams span 390 seconds.
6. Audio signal is non-silent and below clipping threshold.
7. Deliverables include MP4, SRT, transcript, generated contact sheet, and machine-readable manifest.

Automated checks establish technical integrity, not artistic acceptance. A full visual review and full-length listening review remain required before final publication.
